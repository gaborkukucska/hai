## 🌟 What is HAI-Net?

HAI-Net represents a fundamental reimagining of human-AI collaboration through a **decentralized, privacy-first framework**. It creates an unbreakable bond between local AI entities and their human counterparts while ensuring privacy, security, and shared prosperity through innovative resource sharing and community building.
